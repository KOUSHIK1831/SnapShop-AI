## Introduction

SnapShop AI is a platform designed to empower local vendors to reach their community digitally. It features multilingual support (English, Telugu, Hindi) and AI-driven digitization of physical inventory.

## Getting Started for Sellers

- **Sign up/Login**: Use your email to create a seller profile.
- **Store Setup**: Configure your store name, category, and banner.
- **Add Inventory**: Use the "Digitize" feature to upload photos of your price lists or physical items. Our AI will automatically extract product details.

## Buying on SnapShop AI

- **Browse Stores**: Find local vendors in your area.
- **Select Products**: Add items to your cart.
- **Checkout**: Complete your order with your preferred contact information.

## Language Support

SnapShop AI supports English, Telugu, and Hindi. Use the language switcher in the navigation menu to change your preference.

## Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues:

1. Refresh the application.
2. Check your network connection.
3. Use the Feedback modal to report bugs.
