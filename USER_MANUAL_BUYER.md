## 1. Creating an Account

1. Open the SnapShop AI website.
2. Click **"Get Started"** or **"Sign In"** on the homepage.
3. On the Auth page, click **"Create Account"** (or **"Sign Up"** tab).
4. Select your role — choose **Buyer** (Shopping Bag icon).
   > ⚠️ Your role is permanent and cannot be changed later.
5. Fill in the registration form:
   - **Phone**: Enter your 10-digit mobile number (starts with +91).
   - **Full Name**: Your name as it should appear on orders.
   - **Email**: A valid email address (used for login and verification).
   - **Password**: Minimum 6 characters.
6. Click **"Create Account"**.
7. You will be redirected to email verification (see next section).

---

## 2. Email Verification (OTP)

1. After signing up, an OTP (One-Time Password) is sent to your email.
2. Enter the **6-digit code** displayed on the verification screen.
3. If you don't see the email, check your **Spam/Promotions** folder.
4. You can request a resend:
   - First resend: wait **60 seconds**
   - Second resend: wait **180 seconds**
   - Third resend: wait **300 seconds**
   - Maximum 3 resend attempts.
5. On successful verification, you will be redirected to your **Buyer Dashboard**.

## 3. Logging In

1. Go to the SnapShop AI website and click **"Sign In"**.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Click **"Sign In"**.
4. You will be redirected to your **Buyer Dashboard**.

### Forgot Password?

1. On the login page, click **"Forgot Password?"**.
2. Enter your **Email address**.
3. Check your email for an OTP code.
4. Enter the OTP on the screen.
5. You will be redirected to the **Reset Password** page.
6. Enter your **new password** (minimum 6 characters) and confirm it.
7. Click **"Update Password"**.
8. After success, you will be redirected back to the login page.

### Login Troubles

- **Too many failed attempts**: After 5 failed login attempts, you will be locked out for **15 minutes**.
- **Account blocked**: If your account was blocked by an admin, you will see a message with contact details.

## 4. Browsing Stores & Products

### Buyer Dashboard

After logging in, you will see the Buyer Dashboard with three tabs:

| Tab         | Description                  |
| ----------- | ---------------------------- |
| **Shops**   | Browse all approved stores   |
| **Orders**  | View your order history      |
| **Profile** | View account info & sign out |

### Exploring Shops

1. Go to the **Shops** tab.
2. Browse the grid of store cards. Each card shows:
   - Store banner and icon
   - Store name and location
   - Short description
   - Product categories available
   - Number of products
3. **Search stores**: Use the search bar to find stores by name or location.
4. **Filter by category**: Click on category chips to narrow down stores.
5. Click **"Visit Store"** on any store card.

### Public Storefront

The storefront page shows a store's full catalog. No login is required to browse.

Each product card shows:

- Product image
- Title and description
- Price (with any discount shown)
- Available sizes (for size-variant products)
- Stock availability
- **"Order Now"** button
- **WhatsApp inquiry** button (to message the seller)

Use the **category filter pills** at the top to browse specific product categories within the store.

## 5. Placing an Order

1. Browse to a product you want to buy.
2. Select a **size** (if applicable).
3. Click **"Order Now"**.
   - If you are not logged in, a login/signup popup will appear.
   - If you are a seller account, you cannot place orders.
4. The **Order Confirmation** dialog opens with three steps:

### Step 1 — Delivery Address

Fill in the following:

- **Full Name**
- **Phone** (10-digit number)
- **Address** (street, area, building)
- **City**
- **State**
- **Pincode** (6 digits)

Review the order summary (product, price) at the top. Click **"Continue to Payment"**.

### Step 2 — Choose Payment Method

- **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: Pay cash when your order arrives.
- **Online Payment**: Choose from options provided by the seller:
  - **UPI QR**: Scan and pay directly to the seller's UPI ID.
  - **Razorpay**: Pay via cards, netbanking, UPI, or wallets.

Click **"Continue"** after selecting your method.

### Step 3 — Complete Payment

#### For COD:

Click **"Place Order"** — the order is placed. No upfront payment needed.

#### For UPI:

1. Scan the **QR code** shown using any UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm).
2. Complete the payment on your UPI app.
3. (Optional) Upload a **payment proof screenshot**.
4. Click **"I have paid"** to confirm.
5. The seller will verify the payment before processing your order.

#### For Razorpay:

1. The Razorpay checkout window opens.
2. Complete the payment using your preferred method.
3. On success, the order is placed automatically.

### Order Confirmation

After successful placement, you will see:

- **Order summary** (product, amount, payment method, delivery address)
- **Order ID** for reference
- WhatsApp button to contact the seller

## 6. Making Payment

| Method               | How It Works                                          | When Payment Is Collected |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| **Cash on Delivery** | Pay cash to the delivery person                       | On delivery               |
| **UPI QR**           | Scan QR & pay via GPay/PhonePe/Paytm                  | At the time of ordering   |
| **Razorpay**         | Pay via card/UPI/netbanking through Razorpay checkout | At the time of ordering   |

For UPI and Razorpay, payment is collected immediately. For COD, you pay only when the order is delivered.

## 7. Tracking Your Order

### From Buyer Dashboard

1. Go to the **Orders** tab.
2. Click **"Track Order"** on any order card.

### Track Order Page

You will see:

- **Order summary card**: Product image, title, price, order ID, status badge, placed date
- **Progress timeline**: Visual step-by-step progress bar showing:
  ```
  Order Placed → Preparing → Ready → Shipped → Out for Delivery → Delivered
  ```
- **Current status** is highlighted. Completed steps are marked green.
- If the order is **cancelled**, a cancellation notice is shown instead.

### Delivery Details

When the order is shipped, you will see:

- **Courier tracking**: Provider name + tracking number with a link to the courier's tracking page.
- **Self-delivery**: The delivery person's name and phone number (tap to call).

### Activity Log

A timeline showing every status change with date/time stamps.

> Orders update in real-time — you don't need to refresh the page.

## 8. Viewing Order History

1. Go to the **Orders** tab on the Buyer Dashboard.
2. All your orders are listed with:
   - Product image and title
   - Current status (color-coded badge)
   - Quantity
   - Payment method and payment status
   - Total price
   - Action buttons: **Track Order**, **Visit Store**

### Order Statuses

| Status                 | Meaning                           |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| **Pending**            | Seller has received your order    |
| **Preparing**          | Seller is preparing your items    |
| **Ready for Dispatch** | Order is packed and ready         |
| **Shipped**            | Order has been handed to delivery |
| **Out for Delivery**   | Delivery person is on the way     |
| **Delivered**          | Order has been delivered to you   |
| **Cancelled**          | Order was cancelled               |

### Payment Statuses

| Status                    | Meaning                              |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Pending**               | Payment not yet made/received        |
| **Paid**                  | Payment received                     |
| **Awaiting Verification** | UPI payment — seller needs to verify |
| **Failed**                | Payment failed                       |
| **Refunded**              | Payment refunded                     |

## 9. Profile & Settings

1. Go to the **Profile** tab on the Buyer Dashboard.
2. You can view:
   - Your name, email, and avatar
   - Your role badge (**Buyer**)
3. The only action available is **"Sign Out"**.

> Note: Buyers cannot edit their profile information. Contact the store seller for any changes.

---

## 10. Logging Out

1. Go to the **Profile** tab (bottom navigation).
2. Click the **"Sign Out"** button.
3. You will be signed out and redirected to the homepage.

You can also log out by closing your browser — sessions expire automatically after a period of inactivity.

## 11. Troubleshooting

| Problem                           | Solution                                                                            |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Can't log in                      | Use **Forgot Password** to reset. Wait 15 min if locked out.                        |
| OTP not received                  | Check Spam folder. Wait for resend cooldown.                                        |
| Order stuck on "Pending"          | Contact the seller via WhatsApp button on the order.                                |
| Payment not reflecting            | Wait 5 minutes. If still not updated, contact the seller with your payment proof.   |
| Account blocked                   | Contact admin at the email shown in the alert message.                              |
| Language not displaying correctly | Use the Language Switcher (globe icon) to switch between English, Hindi, or Telugu. |
| Can't place order                 | Ensure your email is verified. Sellers cannot place orders.                         |
